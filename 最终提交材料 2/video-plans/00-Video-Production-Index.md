## Common Recording Requirements

- Resolution: 1920x1080 HD.
- Camera: the current speaker must be visible in speaker view.
- Team participation: every team member must make a meaningful contribution if the project has more than one member.
- Language: English unless the lecturer has explicitly approved another language.
- Audio: clear voice, no clipping, background noise, or notification sounds.
- Screen: code, diagrams, terminal output, and metrics must remain readable at 100% playback size.
- Privacy: hide passwords, tokens, email addresses, private URLs, and personal notifications.
- Evidence: use real application, repository, CI, test, and security results. Clearly label future architecture as not implemented.
- Editing: remove waiting time, dependency downloads, long builds, and repeated explanations.
- Backup: keep the editable recording project and the final MP4 separately.
